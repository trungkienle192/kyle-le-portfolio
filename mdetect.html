Cracking America for a Muon-Physics Startup

mDetect builds EduMOD, a desktop device that lets a high-school physics class watch cosmic-ray muons hit their own classroom, no lab required. What it didn't have was a way into American schools. Our six-person team spent 12 weeks finding one — for a real client, presenting to real stakeholders.

The diagnosis

mDetect relied almost entirely on cold emails and trade-show demos. No dedicated social presence, no inbound channel — a hard way to earn a school district's trust.

At USD $3,392.50, EduMOD was priced against CosmicWatch and UKRAA — US-made alternatives that undercut it by an order of magnitude.

US wholesalers stock science kits at all

Only 15.3% of school-supply wholesalers in the US carry scientific equipment — the distribution channel mDetect needed barely exists.

Field note — that last stat is what changed our recommendation. We stopped looking for a distributor to sign and started planning a subsidiary to build.

Exhibit B

Who we were actually selling to

Robert H. Gryphon
District STEM Director, Palo Alto Unified · our primary buyer persona

"Show me it works in someone else's classroom first, and show me the board what this costs over five years, not just the invoice."

Motivated by

Measurable learning outcomes, equity-driven adoption, long-term ROI he can defend to the board.

Blocked by

Tight budgets, proposals without pilot data, and the need for stakeholder consensus before any purchase.

Exhibit C

The strategy: product, price, place

01 · Product

Ship the app EduMOD never had

Turn a hardware device into a curriculum platform

  • Dedicated learning app with gamified progression and adaptive difficulty
  • New geology & earth-science modules aligned to NGSS and the California Science Framework
  • Positions EduMOD as a plug-and-play, standards-aligned STEM solution, not just an instrument
02 · Price

Sell the way schools actually buy

Districts don't write $3,400 cheques — they lease

  • Three tiers: direct sale, 1-year lease, 2-year lease-to-own
  • Break-even at $2,121 per unit against a $3,392.50 price point
  • Mirrors how public schools actually procure equipment
≈60% gross margin, Year 1
03 · Place

Stop chasing distributors — become one

A San Jose subsidiary, not another wholesaler pitch

  • California LLC: 5 full-time staff, 1 technician, 1 delivery driver
  • Direct-to-district sales, bypassing a wholesale channel that barely exists
  • Segmented email (+760% revenue lift), handwritten letters (+50% recall) and PTA "STEM Family Night" events
