The route

Three regions, three different roads

Stop 1 — North America

The home turf, already crowded

The USA alone brings in $45M+ in gross sales, led by fleet buyers. But it's a saturated lane, sharing the road with Tesla, Chevrolet and Ford.

Stop 2 — Europe

A slow lane with room to move

The Netherlands leads, Denmark trails at just $3.8M — EVs are still under 3% of total car sales here. Plenty of open road, if the incentives hold.

Stop 3 — Asia

The on-ramp just opened

Indonesia rivals Europe's top market, and its EV tariff just dropped to zero. China, the world's biggest EV market, is still untouched by SparkEV.

The road ahead

An 8-quarter forecast into 2025

Tableau workbook · Forecast Analytics

North America — downhill

The region that got SparkEV here is forecast to lose sales and margin into 2025 as the market saturates and battery-supply costs bite.

Europe — uphill, but climbing

From $0.85M in gross sales in 2019 toward a forecast $2M+ by early 2023, backed by incentives and fast-growing charging infrastructure.
